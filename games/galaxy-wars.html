About The Game

The alien invasion has begun — Earth is under attack, and the fight for humanity’s survival has started. Step into an AR FPS alien shooter experience unlike any other.

🌍 Immersive Ground Battles

Fight through Earth’s destroyed cities under siege. Enemies use cover, flank your position, and coordinate attacks like real soldiers. Equip pistols, assault rifles, shotguns, and sniper rifles or aim your space cannon — every shot counts in this war for survival.

🚀 Epic Space Combat Missions

Take control of powerful space cannons aboard human battleships and face alien squadrons in cinematic space battles. Fight enemy destroyers, dodge orbital strikes, and experience the chaos of war across the galaxy.

🎨 Anime-Style Storytelling

Enjoy a manga-inspired narrative that tells the story of humanity’s fall. Each mission reveals a new chapter of Earth’s desperate defense, blending comic-style storytelling with intense shooter action.
